# Discover: evidence-archive

> Owner: product — what the business needs and why. No technology here: the
> design says how.

## Context

The evidence of a change — each requirement before and after — is only as
good as the habit of taking it. Testing it on a real project on 2026-11-27,
nothing asked for the page, nothing said a requirement had been delivered
with no before, and the page compared with whatever base the clock gave it.

## Use case

The Developer closes a change and its evidence closes with it: the page is
written as the change is archived, a requirement delivered with no before is
named, and a team that wants every before can require it.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-1: archiving a change whose scenarios run writes its evidence page, current, into what is archived.
- AC-2: a requirement delivered with no before is named at archive; a team that requires the before is refused, unless it forces.
- AC-3: the person chooses what the page compares with.
